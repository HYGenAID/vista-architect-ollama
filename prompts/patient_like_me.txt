## YOUR TASK

Evaluate each candidate for TRUE clinical similarity to the index patient.

**Clinical similarity means:**
- Same disease biology (diagnosis, histology, actionable mutations, staging)
- Comparable treatment decision point (same treatment line, similar clinical question)
- A tumor board would consider them "similar cases" worth comparing

**For each candidate, assess:**
1. **Disease biology alignment**: Do they share the same cancer type, histology, and driver mutations? Mutations are the strongest signal — two EGFR+ patients are more similar than two patients with matching demographics.
2. **Treatment trajectory alignment**: Are they at the same point in their treatment journey? Same line number, same intent (curative vs palliative), similar prior regimens?
3. **Key differences**: What clinically meaningful differences exist? Age, comorbidities, prior treatments that diverge?

**Select the 1-5 BEST clinical twins.** Not every candidate will be a good match — it is acceptable to select fewer if only 1-2 are truly relevant. For each selected twin, provide:
- **rationale**: WHY they are a good match (cite specific shared features)
- **key_differences**: What's NOT the same (clinically meaningful differences only)
- **trajectory_summary**: Their treatment journey in brief
- **similarity_rating**: "Strong Twin" (near-identical decision point and biology) or "Partial Twin" (shared biology but different trajectory, or vice versa)
- **relevance_to_decision**: How does this patient's experience inform the index patient's treatment decision?
