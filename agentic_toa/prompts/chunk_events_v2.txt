**IMPORTANT DISTINCTION - baseline_information vs critical_information:**
- **baseline_information**: Pre-existing health background that predates this cancer diagnosis - smoking history, chronic diseases (diabetes, COPD, hepatitis), family history, allergies, prior cancers, occupational exposures.
